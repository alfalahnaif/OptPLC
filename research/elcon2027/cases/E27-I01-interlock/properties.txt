E27-I01 Engineer-Approved Safety Properties

P-I1 Guard safety
G(!Guard_Closed -> !Machine_Enable)

P-I2 Emergency-stop safety
G(!E_Stop -> !Machine_Enable)

P-I3 External fault safety
G(Fault_Input -> !Machine_Enable)

P-I4 Two-hand requirement
G(!(TwoHand_Left & TwoHand_Right) -> !Machine_Enable)

P-I5 Reset-dependent fault retention
G((Fault_Latched & Safety_Healthy & !Reset_PB) -> X Fault_Latched)

Safety_Healthy =
Guard_Closed AND E_Stop AND NOT Fault_Input

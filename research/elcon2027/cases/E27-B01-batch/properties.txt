E27-B01 Engineer-Approved Properties

P-B1 Emergency-stop safety
G(!E_Stop -> !(Fill_Valve | Mixer_Motor | Drain_Valve))

P-B2 Abort safety
G(Abort -> !(Fill_Valve | Mixer_Motor | Drain_Valve))

P-B3 Fill/drain mutual exclusion
G(!(Fill_Valve & Drain_Valve))

P-B4 Mixer state restriction
G(Mixer_Motor -> Step_State = 2)

P-B5 Reset-dependent fault retention
G((Fault_Latched & Safety_Healthy & !Reset_PB) -> X Fault_Latched)

Safety_Healthy = E_Stop AND NOT Abort
